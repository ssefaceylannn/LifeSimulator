### Big surprises / caveats
- **BIST 100 is only ~+2,8% YTD** (21 Oct) despite being ~+25% at mid-year — big H2 drawdown.
- **Bedelli askerlik jumped 3× in one year**: ₺333k (Jan) → ₺416k (Apr law change) → ₺472,7k (Jul). Dövizle askerlik is now TL-denominated at the same amount (≈ €8.500), not a fixed €5.000/€2.000.
- **US**: new $250 Visa Integrity Fee makes B1/B2 ≈ $435; the $100k H-1B fee was extended to Sep 2027 but is currently blocked in court.
- **Pasaport**: harç rose only 18,95% (not 25,49%); your ₺14.761 for 10 years is correct.
- **Ehliyet ≈ ₺35–42k** first-try total.
- Berlin rents are the only major-metro rents falling (−0,8% YoY).
